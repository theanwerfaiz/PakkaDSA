# Code of Conduct

We want PakkaDSA to be welcoming to learners and contributors of every background. Be kind, be patient, assume good intent, and focus on what helps students learn. Harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact details on their GitHub profile.

Adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
